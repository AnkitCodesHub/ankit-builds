FAQ

Questions business owners ask

Something else on your mind? Get in touch and ask directly.

How much does a business website cost?

Websites start at ₹4,999 (Starter), ₹9,999 (Professional) and ₹14,999 (Advanced with booking or ordering). The final price depends on pages, features and integrations, and you get a written quotation before work starts.

How long does website development take?

It depends on the scope and how quickly content (text, photos, logo) is ready. Smaller websites usually take a few weeks; booking or ordering projects take longer. The timeline is agreed in writing in your quotation.

Are domain and hosting charges included?

No. Domain registration, hosting and other third-party services are paid separately at actual cost. I can help you choose and set them up.

Can I request custom website features?

Yes. Tell me what you need during the consultation, and custom features are included in your quotation.

Will the website work on mobile devices?

Yes. Every website is designed mobile-first and tested on phones, tablets and desktops.

Can customers contact my business through WhatsApp?

Yes. WhatsApp integration is included in every package, so customers can message you with one tap, with a ready-written enquiry where useful.

Do you provide website maintenance?

Yes. Support after launch is available according to the agreed scope, and ongoing maintenance can be arranged as a monthly plan quoted per website.

Can you integrate online payments?

Yes. Payment gateway integration is available with the Advanced package. Gateway providers charge their own transaction fees separately.

Do you provide social media management?

Yes. Instagram and Facebook management, creatives, reels and a monthly report. It's priced by quotation after understanding your business and posting needs.

How do I start a project?

Send a message or fill in the enquiry form below. We'll have a short consultation, then you'll receive a quotation with scope and timeline.

What is the payment process?

The price is agreed before work starts. Usually 50% is paid upfront to begin and the remaining 50% before launch.

Will I own my website and domain?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name, and once the project is paid in full, the website and its accounts are yours.
