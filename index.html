FAQ

Questions clients usually ask

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope, and you'll get an exact timeline in the proposal before we start. As a rough guide: a landing page takes about 1 week, a business website 2–3 weeks, and an app MVP 4–8 weeks.

How does payment work?

We agree on a fixed price before any work starts. I take 50% upfront to begin and the remaining 50% before launch. No hourly billing, no surprise costs.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. I work remotely with clients anywhere, over email, WhatsApp and video calls, and I can schedule calls to overlap with your working hours.

Can you handle the domain, hosting and launch?

Yes. I can set up your domain, hosting and business email, deploy the site or app, and keep it running afterwards with a monthly care plan if you want one.

Will I own the website or app?

Yes. Once the project is paid in full, the code, design and accounts are yours. Nothing is locked to me.

How quickly do you reply?

Within 24 hours, usually much sooner. Once we're working together, you get regular progress updates without having to chase me.
